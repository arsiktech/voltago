Effective 16 July 2026 · TestFlight beta

These terms supplement the Apple TestFlight terms that apply to your beta access.

Purpose

Volta Go is pre-release software for evaluating a free-first, low-cost EV charging decision aid in Spain. Features, data coverage, and availability may change or stop during the beta.

Safe use

Do not use or interact with the app while driving. Stop safely before reading results or changing a route. Obey road signs, parking rules, site access rules, charger instructions, and applicable law.

Verify before relying

Charger availability, price, connector compatibility, access, and parking can change after the app checks them. Volta Go separates live, official, directory, and community evidence and links to public sources, but it cannot guarantee a charger will be usable. Confirm important details with the operator before making a journey or purchase.

No charging transaction

Volta Go does not operate chargers, start sessions, take payment, or contract for charging or parking. Any transaction or dispute is between you and the relevant operator or venue.
