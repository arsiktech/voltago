What Volta Go does

Volta Go brings together charger information from public and open sources, such as public registries, operator maps and open directories, and shows where each fact came from. Place search works worldwide. Charger coverage, price information and live status depend on the sources available for each area. Coverage is strongest in Spain, and some areas have few or no listed chargers.

Each charger shows how its information is known. "Live" means it comes from a source that reports current status; the app marks it "Stale" or "Limited live" when that report is old or incomplete. "Official" means an operator or public authority published it. "Directory" means a public charger directory or map lists it. "Community" means it comes from community-maintained data, such as OpenStreetMap, or from driver reports. Only Live information describes current status, and even Live status can change before you arrive.
