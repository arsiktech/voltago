Privacy

Effective 10 November 2026

Volta Go is an independent beta charging decision aid. No Volta Go account is required. It has no advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Information the app handles

  • Location: only when you grant permission, to find chargers near you and calculate distance. You can use city search instead.
  • Search and map area: a city query or visible map bounds are sent to the Volta Go service so it can return nearby results.
  • Your EV: on the web, battery, range and capacity are readings you type, and a car photo you choose stays in this browser. Volta Go does not upload those readings or that photo. The example car is sample data in this browser. Live connection is unavailable unless this service has vehicle connection enabled. When it is enabled, Connect your car opens Smartcar for read-only approval. The service can then receive make, model, year, battery, range, charging state and usable capacity where Smartcar supplies them. It does not receive your car-brand password, VIN or vehicle location, and it cannot unlock, drive or start charging. Charging power is not read. Provider tokens stay in service memory for that browser session and are removed on disconnect or when the service restarts. The browser keeps an HttpOnly session cookie. When vehicle connection is not enabled, none of that Smartcar exchange happens. The iPhone app uses the same switch: it stores only its Volta session in the Keychain, and only after vehicle connection is enabled.
  • Saved chargers: on the web, Sign in is shown only when you save a charger or open Saved. That choice, plus the charger names and IDs you save, stays in this browser. Volta Go does not create an account and does not upload the list.
  • Manual readings and car photos: details and photos you enter remain in your browser or the iPhone app's local storage. Choosing a car photo does not upload it to the Volta Go service.
  • Network metadata: the service may receive your IP address and basic request metadata for delivery, reliability, abuse prevention, and rate limiting. Charger searches are logged without their query string, so precise coordinates and city search text are excluded from those access-log lines.
  • AI assistants: if you connect Volta Go to an AI app such as ChatGPT or Claude, that app sends only the search it decides to make: a place name or coordinates, filters, or a charger ID. ChatGPT may also send your approximate city; Volta Go uses only the city, region and country text, never coordinates from that hint. These requests need no account, are answered from the same charger data, and are logged without their content. The AI app's own privacy terms cover your conversation.
  • Support messages: if you email us, we receive the address, content, and attachments you choose to send.

Public services and sources

Apple MapKit may load map content in the iOS app. The web map loads styles, fonts and tiles directly from OpenFreeMap; those requests reveal your IP address and the map area being viewed. City search can use OpenStreetMap Nominatim through the Volta Go service. Charger facts come from attributed public sources such as MapaREVE and public operator pages. Their own privacy terms apply when your device opens an external source or map link.

Use, sharing, and retention

Information is used only to provide nearby charger results, secure and operate the beta, diagnose failures, and answer support. We do not sell personal information. Data can be handled by the infrastructure providers needed to deliver the service and email. The app keeps no account profile or location history database. Live request state is cached in memory and disappears when the service restarts; infrastructure access logs and support email remain only under the relevant host or mailbox retention controls and for as long as reasonably needed for security, troubleshooting, or the support conversation.

Your choices

You can type vehicle readings or search for chargers without adding a car. Removing a manual EV removes its saved readings and photo. Clearing this browser's storage removes those readings, the photo and saved chargers. When vehicle connection is enabled, disconnect removes that session from the service. Revoking Smartcar or car-brand access, if a grant exists, is done in that provider’s account.

You can deny or revoke location permission in iOS Settings, use city search, stop using the beta, or delete the app. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you sent directly, email supportvoltago.app. We will respond subject to applicable law and the technical limits described above.

Changes and contact

This notice may change as the beta evolves. The effective date above will change when it does. Privacy and security questions: supportvoltago.app.
