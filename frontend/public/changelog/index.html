Changelog

Recent changes to Volta Go, newest first

13 November 2026

  • Web app: the browser's own controls and scrollbars now follow the light or dark theme you choose in the app.

11 November 2026

  • Web app: added a Feedback button for sending a short note and, if you choose, a screenshot of the page.
  • Three more public charger directories are now included, each attributed to its source.

6 November 2026

  • You can browse and search without signing in. "Sign in" appears only when you save a charger or open Saved, and the place you chose stays in the search box.
  • Place search finds more named places. When a place in a town is not found, Volta Go shows the town and says so.
  • Slow AC sockets that OpenStreetMap lists in watts are no longer shown as high-power chargers.
  • The source coverage page shows "Unknown" instead of "Not listed" when a source's list has not been fetched recently.

24 October 2026

  • New Spanish pages list chargers by city at voltago.app/cargadores. Each price shows its source and date.
  • Official charger registries from Catalonia, Navarra and Castilla y León now confirm existing chargers and add new ones. They carry no prices or live status.
Volta Go is free. It finds and compares chargers; it does not start or pay for charging.
