Support

Help for the iPhone app, the Android app and the web app

Email supportvoltago.app for help, to report a wrong charger, or to make a privacy request. In the web and Android apps you can also use the Feedback button when it is shown.

What to include in a report

  • Which app you use: iPhone, Android or web.
  • On iPhone, the Volta Go version and build number shown under About in the app's own Settings screen, and your iOS version.
  • On Android or the web, your phone or computer model and your browser.
  • The date, the approximate time and the city or area.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Crop out your home address, number plates, faces and anything else private.

Never send passwords, payment card details or charging-app logins.

Report a wrong charger

If a charger is missing, closed, priced differently or in the wrong place, email us with:

  1. The charger name and address as Volta Go shows it, or the source link from its details.
  2. The label on the charger: Live, Official, Directory or Community.
  3. What Volta Go showed, and what is actually true.
  4. How you know: the operator's app or website, a sign on site, or your own visit, with the date.

Volta Go shows information as its sources publish it, so we may also need to ask the source to correct it. In the iPhone app, you can tell other drivers what happened with "I charged", "Couldn't charge" or "Occupied" on the charger's details.

What the labels mean

  • Live: from a source that reports current status. "Stale" or "Limited live" means that report is old or incomplete.
  • Official: published by an operator or a public authority.
  • Directory: listed by a public charger directory or map.
  • Community: from community-maintained data, such as OpenStreetMap, or from driver reports.

Only Live describes current status. Check the operator's app, website or on-site signs before you rely on any charger.

Location does not work

  • iPhone: open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Location Services, then Volta Go, and choose "While Using the App".
  • Android: allow location for Volta Go in Android settings, and for voltago.app in Chrome's site settings.
  • Web: allow location for voltago.app in your browser's site settings.

You can always search for a city or place instead.

Privacy and data requests

Volta Go has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. To access, correct or delete information we may hold about you:

  1. Email supportvoltago.app with the subject "Privacy request".
  2. Say what you want: a copy, a correction or deletion.
  3. Tell us how to find it: the dates and times you used Volta Go, your IP address then if you know it, the time you sent feedback, or the email address you wrote from.

We answer within one month. Data on your device is in your control: remove saved chargers and readings in the app, clear this site's data in your browser, or delete the app. To end a car connection, use Disconnect in "Your EV". The Privacy Notice explains what we process and for how long.

Safety

Do not use Volta Go while driving. Volta Go is not an emergency or roadside-assistance service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
