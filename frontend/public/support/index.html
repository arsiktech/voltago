Support

Volta Go TestFlight beta

For help or beta feedback, email arsik2121icloud.com. Support is best effort during the beta.

Before reporting a charger

Open the station card and note whether Volta Go labels it Live, Official, Directory, or Community. If it is safe to do so, confirm the operator page and on-site signage. Never send payment credentials or a charging-app login.

What to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The Volta Go version and build number shown in TestFlight.
  • The station name or public source link, the label shown, and what happened.
  • Steps to reproduce a crash, blank screen, map issue, or accessibility problem.

Please remove passwords, payment information, vehicle identifiers, and exact home-location details from screenshots or messages.

Privacy and participation

Read the privacy notice and beta terms. You can leave the beta at any time by deleting the app or turning off automatic TestFlight updates.

Volta Go is not emergency or roadside-assistance software.
