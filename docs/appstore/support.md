## Common questions

### Why does Volta say “Not live” or “Verify first”?

Volta keeps live status separate from directory, official-policy, community, and stale
evidence. “Not live” means the station may be useful, but Volta does not have current
public live proof. Check the linked provider source and onsite signs before routing or
charging.

### Why did “Open now” return no results?

Open now fails closed. If current, route-ready availability cannot be verified, Volta
shows zero ready options rather than treating an uncertain or stale station as available.

### Location is not working

In iOS Settings, open Privacy & Security → Location Services → Volta and allow location
while using the app. After permission is granted, Volta requests one location at app
launch to load and rank nearby chargers, and requests it again when you tap the locate
control. Coordinates go to the configured Volta service. You can decline permission and
search for a city or place instead.

### The service is unavailable

Check your connection and retry. A release build needs the operator's configured public
HTTPS service. If the problem persists, send the time, app build, and broad area to
`[SUPPORT EMAIL]`; do not send credentials or an exact residential location.

### A charger price, parking rule, photo, or status is wrong

Provider apps, operator pages, posted parking rules, and onsite signs are authoritative.
Send the station name, linked source, what Volta showed, and what the authoritative source
showed to `[DATA CORRECTION EMAIL OR FORM]`. Do not include faces, number plates, or other
personal information in a photo.

### Restore or manage Volta Plus

If Volta Plus is enabled, use **Restore Purchases** on the paywall for a subscription
already associated with the same Apple ID. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → Apple
Account → Subscriptions. Apple handles payment and refunds. For billing help, use
[Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/billing).

## Accessibility

Report VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, contrast, motion, focus, or touch-target problems to
`[ACCESSIBILITY CONTACT]`. Include the app build, iOS version, accessibility settings,
and the exact screen/control where the problem occurs.

## Safety

Do not interact with Volta while driving. Stop safely before checking a station, source,
or route. In an emergency, contact local emergency services rather than app support.
