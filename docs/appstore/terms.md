## 1. What Volta provides

Volta is a decision-support tool that organizes public and user-verifiable evidence about
electric-vehicle charging. It may show charging price, connector, availability, access,
parking, route, photo, and source information. Volta labels evidence by confidence and
freshness and may hide route-ready claims when current proof is unavailable.

Volta is not a charger operator, parking operator, mapping authority, emergency service,
or payment service for charging. It does not start a charging session or reserve a bay.

## 2. Verify before relying

Charging availability, tariffs, parking prices, access rules, connector condition, and
physical circumstances can change without notice. Provider apps, operator pages, posted
signs, local rules, and onsite conditions are authoritative. A “Go now” decision reflects
the evidence available to Volta at that time; it is not a guarantee that a charger or
parking space will remain available.

You are responsible for confirming the station, price, access, parking, connector, and
route before acting. Do not use Volta while driving. Follow road laws and stop safely
before interacting with the app.
